# Goals and Actions: Tracking Progress

> TL;DR: Goals give readers a target. Actions give them steps. Together, they turn reading into doing — and the progress bar shows how far you have come.

## The Progress Bar

Notice the thin bar at the top of this post? That is your progress indicator. Every action you check off moves it forward. It persists — come back tomorrow and your progress is still here.

[reveal:How does the progress bar work?|It counts all checkboxes on the page (actions and checks). As you tick them off, the bar fills. The state is saved in your browser localStorage, so it survives page refreshes and return visits.]
