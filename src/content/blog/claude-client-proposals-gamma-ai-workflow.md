Creating client presentations used to take days—finding templates, designing slides, and formatting content by hand. Now, with the right AI workflow, you can create professional, client-ready proposals in minutes instead of hours.

## The Problem: Manual Presentation Work is a Time Sink

As business owners, we've all been there. You need to create client proposals, pitch decks, service offerings, or consultation decks. You spend literal days:

- Finding PowerPoint templates that look professional
- Designing each slide individually
- Modifying content to fit your needs
- Only to have the presentation eventually discarded

This is obviously not a good use of time—and it's exactly the kind of repetitive work AI should automate.

## The Solution: Claude + Gamma AI Integration

Brendan Jowett demonstrates his agency's complete workflow for creating client proposals using Claude AI integrated with Gamma AI. The key insight: **context is everything**.

When you just tell Claude "create a presentation" without additional information, it generates generic content—what he calls "AI slop." But when you provide rich context—diagrams, meeting notes, business details—Claude generates client-ready presentations that actually win business.

## Step 1: Connect Gamma AI to Claude

The process starts by connecting Gamma AI as a skill within Claude:

1. Open Claude settings
2. Navigate to the **Connectors** tab
3. Click **Browse Connectors**
4. Search for and enable **Gamma AI** (presentation builder)
5. Log in to your Gamma account (free version available)

Once connected, Claude can automatically detect when you need a presentation and use Gamma to create it.

## Step 2: Gather Context (The Critical Step)

This is where most people fail with AI-generated content. Without context, you get generic output. With context, you get targeted, relevant content.

Brendan recommends three methods for gathering context:

### Method A: Project Diagrams
Export diagrams from tools like **Whimsical** or **Miro** and paste them directly into Claude. This gives the AI exact understanding of what you're building—no guessing, no hallucinations.

### Method B: Voice Dictation
Speak directly to Claude about the project:
- Scope and requirements
- Timeline and deliverables
- Pricing and cost estimates
- Client pain points and benefits

### Method C: Meeting Notes (Fathom AI)
Use AI note-taking tools like Fathom to extract summaries from client meetings. Import these notes to provide detailed, client-specific context.

## Step 3: Add Business Context

Tell Claude about your company so the presentation reflects your brand:

- Company name and location
- Services offered
- Long-term relationship goals (e.g., 12-month roadmap)

This ensures consistency across all proposals and builds professional credibility.

## Step 4: Create the Presentation

Here's an example prompt structure:

> "I'm looking to create a presentation for this AI voice agent project. I've given you an image which is the scope diagram for the project. This is an inbound receptionist system for a physiotherapy company.
>
> This system is going to take 4 weeks to deliver (2 weeks development, 2 weeks live testing) and cost $10,000.
>
> The client currently takes 50 calls per day, which takes 5 hours of their time. Our system will fully automate these calls, allowing staff to focus on more important work. It also runs 24/7, handling calls at 3:00 a.m. better than a human could.
>
> Please include our company on the presentation. Our name is Inflate AI and we're based in Melbourne, Australia. Also add a slide about a 12-month roadmap."

Claude automatically detects the Gamma skill and generates the presentation in minutes.

## Step 5: Review and Refine

Most of the content is "shippable" with only minor tweaks. Review each slide for accuracy, adjust any details, and you're ready to send it to the client.

## Step 6: Branding Consistency (Optional but Powerful)

Create custom themes in Gamma for consistent branding:
- Upload your logo
- Define your company colors
- Set your design preferences

Claude automatically recognizes and uses existing custom themes, ensuring every proposal looks professional and on-brand.

## Real Results: A Signed Client Project

Brendan shows a completed, real client proposal that resulted in a signed project. The 10-slide deck includes:

1. **Main slide** with AI-generated imagery relevant to the client's industry
2. **Challenges page** outlining specific problems the client faces
3. **Solution page** with implementation steps
4. **How it works** visualization showing the system flow
5. **Key features** page highlighting deliverables

7. **Business benefits** with value proposition

Each slide is independently designed for its content type—relevant graphics, clean layouts, professional presentation.

## Why This Works

### 1. Claude Provides Contextual Understanding
Claude retains information about your business from ongoing conversations. The more you interact with it, the better it understands your services, clients, and needs.

### 2. Gamma Provides Professional Design
Gamma is specifically designed for presentations. It creates visually appealing slides with relevant graphics, not just walls of text.

### 3. Integration is Seamless
Once connected, Claude automatically detects when you need a presentation and uses Gamma without you needing to switch tools or export content manually.

## Bottom Line

This workflow transforms proposal creation from a multi-day manual process into a rapid, AI-assisted workflow that produces professional, client-ready presentations. By combining Claude's contextual understanding with Gamma's design capabilities, agencies can consistently create high-quality proposals that win business while maintaining brand consistency across all materials.
