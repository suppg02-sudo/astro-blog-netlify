# UK News Today: January 6, 2026

Welcome to your daily roundup of the most important news stories from across the United Kingdom.

## Weather

🔴 **Snow and Ice Warnings Across UK**
Over 500 schools have been forced to close in Scotland as severe weather continues to impact the country. Temperatures have plunged, with a Norfolk village recording the coldest night of winter so far at -12°C. The Met Office has warned that more heavy snow is expected throughout the week.

## Politics

🔴 **UK Response to Venezuela Situation**
The UK government has taken a cautious stance regarding the US capture of Nicolás Maduro. Prime Minister Keir Starmer has avoided criticism of Donald Trump, stating that the UK "hasn't got full picture" of the situation. Opposition leader Kemi Badenoch has stated that Trump's Venezuela raid was "morally" right.

🔴 **International Order Concerns**
Wes Streeting has warned that the rules-based international order is "disintegrating before our eyes" amid growing global tensions. The government's Chagos deal has suffered what has been described as "humiliating" defeats in the House of Lords, losing four votes.

## Business & Economy

🔴 **Retailers Entering Administration**
High street chains Claire's and The Original Factory Shop are on the brink of entering administration, putting approximately 2,550 jobs at risk. The companies' owner cited "alarming" low Christmas trading figures.

🟢 **Supermarket Christmas Battle**
Aldi and Lidl have grabbed record market share during the Christmas period, while traditional retailers Asda and Co-op have seen their market share slide.

🟢 **Next Exceeds Expectations**
Retailer Next expects profits to top £1.1 billion following a strong festive trading period, boosting confidence in the retail sector.

🔴 **Jaguar Land Rover Sales Slump**
JLR sales have fallen sharply amid the impact of US tariffs and a cyber-attack, creating concerns for the UK automotive industry.

📊 **UK Car Sales**
Despite challenges for some manufacturers, UK car sales topped 2 million units in 2025, with Chinese brands showing significant growth.
