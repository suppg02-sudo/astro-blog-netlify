<h2>AI Agents as Team Members</h2><p>Atlassian has launched an open beta for Agents in Jira, integrating AI agents as full team members in projects. Users can now assign work items to agents based on Atlassian AI assistant Rovo, as well as third-party agents via the Model Context Protocol.</p><h3>How It Works</h3><ul><li><strong>Direct Assignment</strong>: Assign tasks to AI agents like human team members</li><li><strong>Full Integration</strong>: Tasks appear in boards, sprints, and releases</li><li><strong>Status Tracking</strong>: Monitor agent progress alongside human work</li></ul>
