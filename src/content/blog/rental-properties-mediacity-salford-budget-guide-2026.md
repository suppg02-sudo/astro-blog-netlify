## Things to Consider

### Additional Costs

- **Council Tax**: Band B/C typical (~£1,400-1,800/year)
- **Bills**: ~£100-150/month (electricity, gas, water, internet)
- **Transport**: ~£80/month for Metrolink season ticket (if needed)

### Viewing Tips

1. **Check availability dates** - some properties have long lead times
2. **Ask about parking** - not all MediaCity flats include it
3. **Verify bills inclusion** - some student properties include utilities
4. **Visit at different times** - MediaCity can be busy during BBC shift changes
