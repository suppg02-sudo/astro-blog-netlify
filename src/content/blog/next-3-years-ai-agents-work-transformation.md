## The Three-Year Strategic Timeline

### 2025: Agent Swarms

This is happening right now. Think about this in terms of deploying networks of specialized AI agents that work together to solve complex problems. Each agent has its own level of autonomy, creating a digital ecosystem that mirrors how human teams operate—but 100 times faster.

Fortune 500 companies have already started deploying hybrid workforces where human managers oversee both human and AI workers. The key shift will be understanding how to manage different levels of AI autonomy.

### 2026: Physical Agents

Digital intelligence enters the physical world. Humanoid robots ship to customers. Nvidia says they'll solve the robot world model by mid-2026, meaning robots will finally understand how to interact with the physical world like humans do.

These first generation robots will cost around $80,000 for advanced models, but basic utility robots will be available for $2,000 to $10,000—about the price of a used car.

### 2027: Everything Accelerates

This is when AI systems begin improving themselves without human intervention. The feedback loops we've created will accelerate AI development beyond human comprehension. We're not just talking about smarter AI—we're talking about systems that can innovate and evolve in ways we can't even imagine yet.

Every major AI lab has figured this out simultaneously. There is no moat. No single company owns this future. Super intelligent AI won't be controlled by one corporation, which means the advantage goes to whoever deploys these systems first.
