## Troubleshooting Summary

Earlier today, the system was experiencing high CPU load (21.79 load average on 4 cores). The following actions were taken:

### Issues Resolved

1. **Port conflict crash loop** - `opencode-web.service` was repeatedly failing on port 4096 (already in use)
   - **Fix:** Disabled the conflicting service

2. **CGroup limits not applied** - Memory/CPU limits were configured but no processes assigned
   - **Fix:** Updated service file with `MemoryMax=2G` and `CPUWeight=200`
