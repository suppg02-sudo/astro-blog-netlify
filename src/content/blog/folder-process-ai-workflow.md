What if the most transformative way to work with AI isn't about finding the perfect tool or mastering complex workflows? What if it's as simple as an **empty folder**?

Matt Maher introduces "The Folder Process" — a deceptively simple approach that has become his single most significant unlock for working with AI. The concept is so straightforward that it almost sounds trivial, but behind that simplicity lies a paradigm shift in how we approach work with artificial intelligence.

## The Problem: Tool Overload and Analysis Paralysis

Before understanding the solution, we need to recognize the problem it solves. When facing complex work with multiple requirements, we often get stuck. You've probably experienced this: you have people asking for ten different things in ten different places, and you have far too much to deal with.

The result? **You stall.** You get stuck.

But here's the insight that most people miss: **it's not that you don't know what to do — it's that you know too many ways to do it.**

Should you put this in a spreadsheet? Make a to-do list? Organize by person, priority, or timeline? Write something out first? Call somebody? Try out that new tool you've been hearing about?

Every one of those paths leads somewhere genuinely different. Picking one means not picking the others. And it always takes time to figure out if the direction you're going is even the right direction. So you sit there turning it over again and again, and you just stall.

This isn't laziness or personal failure. This happens at every level to everyone. Every single time you face something that doesn't already have a shape to it, you hit the paradox of having **too many tools and not enough clarity** on which one fits this particular problem.

## The Solution: The Folder Process

Here's the entire workflow:

1. **Create a folder** for your project
2. **Add your files** — documents, images, notes, spreadsheets
3. **Talk to AI** about your work
4. **Save what matters** as memory files
5. **Build context** so future conversations require less explanation

That's it. But the power isn't in the simplicity — it's in what this enables.

### Memory Files: The Context Accelerator

When you start a new chat session with AI, it can read a "memory file" first. This means it already knows:
- What project you're working on
- Your budget constraints
- Where you left off last time
- What matters most to you

**You don't have to explain any of that. You just start talking.**

### The Paradigm Shift: Work Defines Tool

For the first time ever, the shape of the work gets to define the tool — not the other way around.

Historically, we've always shaped our work to fit our tools:
- Your team works the way it does because Jira is shaped a certain way
- Your reports look the way they do because the reporting tool is shaped that way

**The tool came first, and then we figured out how to cram our work into it.**

The Folder Process inverts this. The tool gets built around your problem on demand, right now, shaped exactly to what you're doing.

## Real-World Examples

### Home Renovation Project

Matt demonstrates with a home renovation folder containing:
- Contractor bids
- Photos of the space
- A rough budget
- Conversation notes with his wife about what they wanted

He asks AI: *"We've got the contractor's bid back and it's higher than we budgeted. I need to figure out where we can cut scope without losing the things that actually matter to us. The kitchen island, the open floor plan — those were the two things my wife was most firm on. Look through the bid and our budget and our conversation notes and tell me where there's flexibility."*

AI analyzes all the files and provides specific recommendations. Matt saves the output as a file to use in the next contractor meeting.

### Meeting Notes Consolidation

Nine separate meeting notes from the last month get consolidated into one document, organized by date, with a summary at the top — all through a single request.

### Travel Planning

AI checks calendar availability, analyzes hotel options and flight prices, cross-references schedule constraints with budget, and generates personalized recommendations — all by reading the files in the folder.

### Expense Tracking with Automation

This example demonstrates the true power of the approach:

1. **Analysis**: AI identifies budget overages across categories
2. **Integration**: It realizes expense data lives in Google Sheets and writes scripts to pull the data using existing credentials
3. **Visualization**: It builds a custom dashboard showing every category against budget with overages highlighted
4. **Automation**: It sets up a cron job to run the entire process on the first of every month

**Thirty seconds ago, this dashboard didn't exist.** Now it's fully automated.

## The Expertise Model

Here's what makes this different from using a chatbot:

You're not working with one tool — you're working with **a collection of experts**:
- Researchers
- Engineers
- Writers
- Data analysts
- System builders

All from one conversation.

The unlock isn't technical knowledge — it's **willingness to ask**.

You don't have to know:
- How APIs work
- How to write scripts
- How to set up automation

You need to know:
- **What you're trying to accomplish**
- **Be willing to describe it**

The experts figure out the how.

## The Anti-Pattern Warning

When you build something cool inside this process (and you will), **resist the urge to turn it into permanent software** that you try to maintain and ship to other people.

The output here is the value, not any single thing that you build. If you want, hand off the knowledge of *how* it works — that lasts a lot longer than any tool you'll end up creating.

## Getting Started

**Requirements:**
- ChatGPT subscription (for Codex CLI access)
- A folder (literally any folder)
- Your project files
- Willingness to start talking

**No coding required.** All technical work is handled by AI.

## The Bottom Line

You don't need:
- A better tool
- Someone to build the perfect AI shaped exactly like your work
- To master 17 different platforms

You need:
- A folder with some files in it
- The willingness to start talking

The Folder Process is how you start doing real work with AI — not someday, **today**.

**Stop playing at the game of working. Stop wrangling tools. Stop waiting for someone else to build the thing that's going to help.**

Start with an empty folder. Add your files. Have the conversation. Build memory. And discover what it means to work with a collection of experts instead of a chatbot.
