## The Foundation: Claude Code as Your Business Operating System

**What Makes Agentic AI Different:**
- **Access to Multiple Tools**: Can write code, search the web, access files on your computer

## Eight Strategic Workflows That Transform Executive Operations

### 1. Weekly CEO Command: Real-Time Business Intelligence

**The Challenge:** Keeping real-time visibility into business performance while managing multiple priorities.

**The Solution:** Craig runs `/weekly-CEO` every Monday at 8 AM, which:
- Pulls monthly recurring revenue from his analytics platform
- Aggregates churn, customer count, and recent activity metrics
- Reads all business context files (company info, hiring plans, product status, current metrics)
- Presents a comprehensive scorecard in minutes

**The Power:** This isn't just data—it's contextualized intelligence. Once the metrics appear, Craig can have a conversation: *"What caused this drop? What should we do about it based on where we are?"*

Think of it as having a COO or Head of Operations available every Monday morning, delivering exactly what you need to make informed decisions.

### 2. Decision Matrix: Structured Decision-Making Framework

**The Challenge:** Major business decisions need to be data-driven, strategic, and well-reasoned—not gut calls.

**The Solution:** When facing significant decisions, Craig uses the `/decision` command. He describes his question (often using voice transcription):

**Example:** *"I'm considering building a podcast recording and editing tool within Castos. Our customers want an all-in-one solution, and competitors are offering integrated features."*

The system then analyzes the decision through multiple lenses:
- What exactly are we deciding?
- What triggered this decision?
- Timeline and urgency
- Who else is affected?
- Realistic alternatives
- Risk analysis and implications

**Why It Works:** Rather than giving you an answer, it **gives you a framework for thinking**. Like having a trusted advisor who asks clarifying questions instead of lecturing.

### 3. Team Think: People Decisions Using Strategic Analysis

**The Foundation:** The three jobs of every CEO are:
1. Ensure money stays in the bank (profitability)
2. Keep the team aligned on vision and direction
3. Have the right people in the right positions

**The Solution:** The `/team-think` command applies rigorous analysis to hiring and people decisions.

**Example:** Deciding whether to hire a Head of Growth triggers analysis of:
- Should growth be delegated at this stage?
- What's the cost vs. business impact?
- Who manages this person?
- What does the exit math look like?
- Psychological factors: Is this about scaling the business or about the founder's workload?

**Key Insight:** The best businesses attract and develop "AI-forward thinkers"—team members who use AI tools to become exponential amplifiers of their impact.

### 4. Decision Journal: Building Institutional Memory

**The Problem:** Unlike ChatGPT, when you quit Claude Code, the context disappears. Without documentation, you lose the reasoning behind past decisions.

**The Solution:** The `/decision-journal` command creates a permanent record. When you make important decisions, you document them:

**Example:** *"We decided to hire Matt as our marketer, and it's working great. Going forward, I want to track big decisions like this because the right people in the right spots is the key to sustainable growth."*

**The Magic:** The next time you make a decision, Claude Code remembers. It says: *"I remember we hired Matt under similar circumstances. Here's how that decision turned out and how I might think about this new situation."*

This creates an evolving decision-making system that gets smarter as you use it.

### 5. Handoff: The 10-80-10 Delegation Model

**The Concept:** You can't scale by doing everything yourself. The 10-80-10 model works like this:
- **10%**: You define and shape the initial concept
- **80%**: AI or a team member executes
- **10%**: You complete and polish

**The Tool:** Craig runs `/handoff`, which packages everything he's been discussing—all context, goals, success criteria—into a clean markdown file that can be given to:
- A team member for immediate action
- An AI agent for fully automated execution
- A team member + AI for collaborative work

**Real-World Application:**
- New product ideas needing development
- Hiring requirements needing specification
- Features needing detailed requirements
- Projects needing clear ownership

**Business Impact:** You can initiate and shape strategy while delegation handles execution. This is how solo founders scale like larger organizations.

### 6. Meeting Prep: Reducing Cognitive Load

**The Reality:** Modern business involves many meetings, and context is crucial. Do you remember what you discussed with each client last time? What were their action items?

**The Solution:** Craig records all his advisory calls (with permission) and loads transcripts into Claude Code. Five minutes before the next meeting, he asks:

*"I have a meeting with Bob. What did we talk about last time? What action items should he have delivered? What do I need to do to be a good coach?"*

**Why It Works:** Your brain isn't a good storage system for this kind of detail. AI is better. Free up your mental capacity for strategic thinking, active listening, and genuine connection.

### 7. Content Creation Suite: Multipurpose Content Strategy

**The Challenge:** Creating quality content is time-consuming, and repurposing across platforms is tedious.

**Two Creation Modes:**

**A) Original Content:**
- **Essay Command** (`/essay`): "Write something based on this voice note I recorded while walking the dog"
- **Brainstorm Command** (`/brainstorm`): "I like how this creator approached this topic; help me develop my unique angle"

**B) Repurposing Strategy:**
- **Repurpose Command** (`/repurpose`): Takes a single YouTube URL and generates content for three platforms:
  - Substack posts (newsletter format)
  - LinkedIn articles (professional content)
  - Email copy (ConvertKit format)
  
- **Execution Time:** <20 minutes for all three outputs
- **Quality:** Each platform gets optimized content (tone, length, call-to-action appropriate to platform)

**Why This Matters:** One hour of video or deep thinking becomes multi-channel content that reaches different audiences in their native environments—all with platform-specific optimization.

### 8. Systematic Operations: Building Your Command Suite

All of these aren't magic—they're **standardized operating procedures** documented as "skills" or "commands."

**The Architecture:**
- Each recurring task has a defined workflow
- Context files store business knowledge centrally
- Commands are modular and can be combined
- Skills auto-trigger when relevant to a conversation

**The Result:** Your AI learns your business patterns and can anticipate what you need before you ask.

## The Philosophy: AI as Thought Partner, Not Replacement

A crucial distinction runs through all of Craig's workflows: **AI provides perspective, analysis, and execution—but the CEO maintains decision authority.**

> "AI doesn't come up with stuff from scratch for me, but I give it context. I just guide it in the right direction."

This isn't about automation for automation's sake. It's about:
- **Offloading cognitive load** to AI (which is better at memory and pattern recognition)
- **Keeping strategic thinking with the human** (where nuance, intuition, and values matter)
- **Making faster, better-informed decisions** through structured frameworks
- **Documenting everything** so your system gets smarter over time
