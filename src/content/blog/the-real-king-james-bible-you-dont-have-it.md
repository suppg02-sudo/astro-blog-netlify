## Summary

This video by The Real BPEarthWatch discusses the significant changes made to the King James Bible between its original 1611 publication and modern versions. The presenter reveals that over 400 wording changes and approximately 40,000 modifications in spelling, italics, and punctuation have been made to the Old and New Testament scriptures of the King James Bible over the past 400 years.

### Key Points Discussed

- **1611 Original vs Modern Versions**: The King James Bible from 1611 differs substantially from versions printed in the last 200 years
- **Specific Example**: A verse that originally stated "One lamb without blemish" was changed to "One ram without blemish" in later versions
- **Printing History**: Under King James' decree, all Bibles were required to be placed in church pulpits and seminary lecterns throughout England
- **Translation Claims**: Despite prefaces claiming translation "out of the original tongues," modern versions don't accurately reflect the original languages
- **Word Changes**: Terms like "deceive" were changed to "beguile" in certain passages, altering theological meaning
- **Access to Truth**: The presenter recommends obtaining multiple different Bible translations and comparing them to understand the original meanings
