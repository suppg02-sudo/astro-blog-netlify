# Build Your First Interactive Post

> TL;DR: This workshop combines every shortcode into one post. Follow along, check off each step, and by the end you will have the blueprint for your own interactive content.

## Workshop Goal

[goal:workshop|Complete the interactive content workshop|2026-04-08]

## Step 1: Choose Your Post Type

[path:tutorial|How-to / Tutorial|Your post will teach a skill. You need: clear steps (actions), a knowledge check (quiz), and optional depth (reveals). Best for technical content, recipes, guides.]
[path:explainer|Explainer / Deep Dive|Your post will explore a concept. You need: layered depth (reveals), comparisons (compare), and a timeline of context. Best for opinion pieces, analysis, research.]
[path:narrative|Story / Narrative|Your post will tell a story. You need: a timeline for events, reveals for twists, and a quiz for engagement. Best for case studies, personal essays, histories.]

## Step 2: Write Your Title and TL;DR

[reveal:Title formula examples|**Tutorial:** "How to [verb] [thing] in [timeframe]"
**Explainer:** "Why [concept] is [superlative] — and what it means for [audience]"
**Narrative:** "The [adjective] story of [subject]: from [start] to [end]"

Your TL;DR should be one sentence that makes the reader want to continue.]
