When Catherine Austin Fitts — former Assistant Secretary of HUD, former Managing Director at Dillon Read — says $50 trillion has gone missing from the US government, most people assume she's exaggerating. She isn't. The number comes from audited financial disclosures, congressional testimony, and publicly available bailout records. The question isn't whether the money disappeared. The question is: where did it go, and what is it building?

**TL;DR**: The global financial system has a hidden architecture operating above elected governments. Understanding it requires following paper trails, not conspiracy theories. This guide shows you the steps.

## The Mental Model

**One sentence to internalise**: The financial system has two layers — the official story (what politicians tell you) and the real story (where the money actually flows) — and the gap between them is where trillions disappear.

Think of it like a company doing a system migration. You bring up the new system, run it in parallel with the old one, then switch over when ready. Fitts argues that's exactly what's happening at civilisation scale: money is being moved from the old governance structure into a new one, and when the switch happens, you'll find yourself in a digital control grid you didn't consent to.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough

### Step 1: Understand the Numbers

Start with the publicly documented figures:

- **$21 trillion** in undocumentable adjustments at the Department of Defense and HUD between 1998 and 2015 (revealed thanks to financial management laws passed in the first Bush administration that required audited financial statements)
- **$29 trillion** in bailouts during the 2008 financial crisis
- **$5 trillion** injected directly into the economy during the "Going Direct" reset
- Add quantitative easing on top — the total exceeds **$55 trillion**

> [!NOTE] Validation Check
> You can verify the $21 trillion figure yourself. Professor Mark Skidmore at Michigan State University documented these undocumentable adjustments using publicly available data from the DoD and HUD Office of Inspector General reports. Search for "Skidmore undocumented adjustments" — the source documents are government publications.

### Step 2: Map the Players

The system operates through what Fitts calls a "committee system" — real factions with real competition over real resources, but at the top, it's one pot of money. Key institutions:

| Institution | Role |
|-------------|------|
| **Bank of International Settlements (BIS)** | The central bank of central banks. Operates above the law, can hold and move money anywhere in the world secretly |
| **Federal Reserve / Treasury** | Implement domestic monetary policy while the real architecture is built above them |
| **Big investment banks** | Dillon Read, Goldman Sachs et al. — the same firms play the same game globally |
| **"Mr. Global"** | Fitts' term for the network of private financial power operating above elected governments |

The BIS is the critical node. As Fitts describes: central bankers built themselves a bank that is above the law, cannot be audited, and can move money anywhere secretly. During World War II, money moved between American and German interests through the BIS *while the war was being fought*.

### Step 3: See the Pattern Across Countries

Here's the key insight: the same law firms, investment banks, and institutional investors run the same playbook in every country. Fitts discovered this when she presented at a conference alongside journalists covering Eastern European "piratisation" and South American "disaster capitalism":

1. Move people off land (via war, drugs policy, or "redevelopment")
2. Pick up real estate cheap
3. Get government contracts to redevelop
4. Extract the value

She saw it in US housing. The Bolivian peasant women she spoke to saw it in South America. The same contractor was running operations for both. As one woman told her: *"Señorita, you say you know nothing about our country, but from your questions, you know everything about our country."*

### Step 4: Understand the Control Grid

The endgame isn't wealth accumulation for luxury — it's control infrastructure:

- **Digital IDs** — required for all interactions
- **CBDCs (Central Bank Digital Currencies)** — programmable money that can dictate when, where, and on what you can spend
- **Social credit systems** — already operational in China
- **Payment infrastructure** — gives unprecedented visibility into every transaction

As Augustine Carstens of the BIS explained in a publicly available video: the central bank will have "absolute control" over the rules and how they're enforced. He refers to it as "their money" — not yours.

> [!WARNING] Validation Check
> Watch the BIS video for yourself. Search "Augustine Carstens BIS programmable money" — it's 59 seconds and will change how you see digital payments.

### Step 5: Understand Why People Go Along

Fitts' "red button" story explains everything. She asked 100 people at a spiritual conference: *"If you could push a button and stop all hard narcotics trafficking in your community — offending people who control $500 billion to a trillion a year in dirty money — would you push it?"*

**Answer: One person raised their hand.** The rest refused because they didn't want their taxes to go up, their government checks to stop, or their 401(k)s to decline.

The political equation in America since 1980: a politician gives you **your check** and **the story that you are good**. That's it. The "story of I am good" is the marketing package. The real story is systematic extraction.

### Step 6: Take Concrete Action

Fitts outlines four actionable steps from her *Coming Clean* document:

**1. Use cash.** Every analog transaction is a transaction outside the control grid. The grid only locks in at 100% digital.

**2. Bring transparency.** Show people the BIS video. Show them where this leads. Most people simply haven't seen the evidence.

**3. Work locally.** Push your city council, state legislature, or regional government to protect cash infrastructure and prohibit mandatory digital IDs. Idaho already passed a bill preventing the government from requiring digital IDs for any service. Model legislation exists at the Solari Report's Financial Transaction Freedom site.

**4. Stop financing your own prison.** Look at your investments and pension fund. If they're funding the companies building the control grid, move your money. As Fitts says: *"If 10% of the people who are building and financing their own prison would just stop, there'd be a revolution."*

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | What Goes Wrong | What to Do Instead |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Thinking this is a conspiracy theory** | You dismiss verifiable financial data as speculation | Follow the paper trails — start with the Skidmore research and government OIG reports |
| **Believing one country or party is the villain** | You miss the network effect — the same players operate globally | Map the law firms, banks, and institutions. The pattern repeats everywhere |
| **Waiting for a political saviour** | The official story is a marketing package regardless of party | Focus on building local resilience and analog infrastructure instead |
| **Going full prepper alone** | Self-sufficiency in isolation is not viable — humans need community | Organise locally, build coalitions, protect shared infrastructure |
| **Assuming digital = convenient and harmless** | Each digital system you adopt is one more point of control | Evaluate each digital system against where it leads, not just what it does today |

## Generalisation

This pattern — a hidden extraction layer operating above the official system — isn't unique to finance. You see it in:

- **Tech platforms** that offer free services while extracting your data and attention
- **Healthcare systems** where the official story is patient care but the real story is pharmaceutical profit
- **Education** where the narrative is learning but the outcome is debt dependency
- **Media** where the stated purpose is information but the function is narrative control

The meta-pattern is always the same: two layers, an official story and a real story, and the gap between them is where value is extracted. The defence is always the same too: transparency, community organisation, and maintaining analog alternatives.

Fitts frames it simply: *"We have two choices. We can either institute a control grid along with depopulation, or we can use new technology to revitalise the economy bottom-up and create a higher growth rate than our debt rate."*

The game's afoot. Get off the couch.
