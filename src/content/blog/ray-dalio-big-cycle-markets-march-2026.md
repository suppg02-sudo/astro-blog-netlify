# Ray Dalio's Big Cycle Meets Market Reality

*March 2026 | Ray Dalio Analysis + Commodity Markets + UK Financial Conditions*

---

## Ray Dalio's Warning: "Pre-1945 Times"

Bridgewater founder Ray Dalio has published a stark warning: **"The times ahead will be radically different from what most people have gotten used to - they will be more like the tumultuous pre-1945 era than what we have experienced since the end of World War II."**

In his Fortune article and latest Substack post, Dalio identifies **Stage 5 of the Big Cycle** - the period prior to empire collapse - with these hallmarks:

| Hallmark | Current Evidence |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Large/rising debts** | US national debt >$39 trillion |
| **Geopolitical conflicts** | US-Iran war, Strait of Hormuz tensions |
| **Reserve currency concerns** | Gold +70% YoY |
| **Income/wealth gaps** | K-shaped economy widening |
| **Populism rise** | Both left and right populism globally |

---

## The Strait of Hormuz: "The Final Battle"

Dalio's March 16 Substack article frames the Iran conflict as existential:

> *"It all comes down to who controls the Strait of Hormuz... If Iran is left with control over who can pass through the Strait of Hormuz, the United States will be judged to have lost the war."*

**Market Impact of Hormuz Conflict:**

## Five Forces Driving the Big Cycle

Dalio identifies five interrelated forces:

1. **Long-term debt cycle** - Fiat currency debasement
2. **Political cycle** - Internal disorder rising
3. **International geopolitical cycle** - Great power conflict
4. **Technology** - AI disrupting labor markets
5. **Nature** - Climate change acceleration
