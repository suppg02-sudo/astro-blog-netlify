## Storage Tips

1. **Rotate stock**: Use oldest items first
2. **Check dates**: Tinned goods last 2-5 years, dried goods 1-2 years
3. **Pest-proof**: Store dried goods in airtight containers
4. **Cool, dry**: Keep away from heat sources and sunlight

---

## Shopping Strategy

1. **Buy in bulk**: Rice, pasta, flour are cheaper in larger quantities
2. **Check ethnic markets**: Often better prices on rice, spices, tinned goods
3. **Compare brands**: Generic often as good as branded
4. **Look for sales**: Tinned goods often discounted

## Nutritional Notes

This stash provides approximately:
- **Calories**: 2000-2500/day achievable
- **Protein**: 50-80g/day from protein shelf + tinned fish/chicken
- **Carbs**: 250-400g/day from rice, pasta, oats
- **Fats**: 50-80g/day from oils, nuts, dairy
- **Fiber**: 25-35g/day from lentils, veg, oats
- **Vitamins**: Canned veg provide some; consider multivitamin
