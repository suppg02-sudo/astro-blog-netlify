## War Enters Fourth Week

The conflict launched by the United States and Israel against Iran has entered its 22nd day, marking one of the most significant military confrontations in the Middle East in decades. With over 1,444 people killed in Iran alone, including at least 204 children, the humanitarian toll continues to mount as the region faces unprecedented escalation.

## Current Battlefield Status

### Iranian Casualties and Infrastructure Damage

- **Total killed in Iran**: 1,444 (including 204 children)
- **Natanz nuclear facility**: Struck again by US/Israeli forces
- **South Pars gasfield**: Hit by Israeli strikes, affecting Iran's natural gas production
- **Air defences**: Activated over Tehran as country celebrated Nowruz (Persian New Year)

The Iranian armed forces have announced their **70th wave of retaliatory attacks**, launching missiles and drones towards Israel and US bases across the Gulf region.

### Iran's Retaliatory Strikes

Iran has significantly expanded its targeting:

| Target | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Diego Garcia** | 2 ballistic missiles fired at US-UK base in Indian Ocean |
| **Israel** | 9 separate missile salvoes causing widespread damage |
| **Gulf states** | Energy infrastructure targeted across Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Kuwait, UAE |

## Regional Impact

### Gulf States Under Attack

The conflict has spread throughout the Persian Gulf, affecting multiple US-allied nations:

- **Saudi Arabia**: Intercepted 47 drones (38 in just 3 hours over eastern region)
- **Bahrain**: Destroyed 143 missiles and 242 drones total; Patriot systems intercepting drones over residential areas
- **Kuwait**: Mina al-Ahmadi refinery (730,000 barrels/day capacity) hit by Iranian drones, sparking fires
- **UAE**: Threatened with "crushing blows" if aggression launched from UAE territory

### Israel Under Fire

Iranian missile attacks on Israel have intensified:

- Debris and cluster munitions causing widespread damage across central and northern Israel
- **Al-Aqsa Mosque**: Missile fragments fell just 350 metres from the holy site in East Jerusalem
- Palestinian citizens of Israel report disparities in shelter access compared to other residents

## US Position and Escalation

### Trump's Mixed Messages

President Trump has sent conflicting signals about the war's trajectory:

> "I'm considering winding down military operations... but I'm not looking for a ceasefire with Iran."

Despite talk of de-escalation, the US continues to escalate:

- **2,200-2,500 additional Marines** deploying to the Middle East
- **Kharg Island blockade**: US considering occupying Iran's strategic oil hub
- **Potential ground troops**: Reports suggest US boots on ground may be needed to secure Strait of Hormuz

### Strategic Waterway Threats

Trump stated the Strait of Hormuz would need to be "guarded and policed by other countries" that use the vital waterway, shifting responsibility for securing this critical chokepoint through which ~20% of global oil supply passes.

## Wider Regional Conflict

### Lebanon

- **1,001+ killed** since Israel renewed widespread attacks
- At least **118 children** among the dead
- Israel has issued evacuation orders for southern Beirut suburbs

### Syria and Iraq

- Israel struck military infrastructure in **southern Syria** in response to alleged attacks on Druze civilians
- **US logistics camp near Baghdad** attacked by rockets
- Qatar, Jordan, and Egypt condemned the Syrian strikes

## Global Economic Impact

### Oil Markets in Turmoil

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Brent crude** | $112.19 per barrel |
| **Commercial vessel attacks** | 21 confirmed since March 1 |
| **Panama Canal** | Operating at maximum capacity due to LNG tanker demand |

The UK Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) has declared threat levels across the Gulf, Strait of Hormuz, and Gulf of Oman as **"critical"**.

### Energy Policy Contradiction

In a paradoxical move, Washington **lifted sanctions on Iranian oil already loaded on tankers** to boost global supply. Tehran dismissed this as an attempt to give "false hope to buyers," stating they have no surplus crude to sell.

The EU has urged member states to begin storing winter gas, warning that the war has caused "high, volatile" gas prices that could affect storage projections.

## Diplomatic Developments

### UK Involvement

The United Kingdom has allowed the US to use British military bases to carry out attacks on Iranian missile sites. In response, Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi warned that **British lives are in danger**, stating Iran will "exercise its right to self-defence."

### Japan's Unease

Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi met with Trump amid reports of unease in Tokyo after the President cited **Pearl Harbor** to defend the Iran war. Japan sources 90% of its crude from the Middle East and is heavily dependent on exports transiting the Strait of Hormuz. Iran has said it will allow Japanese ships to transit the waterway.

## Humanitarian Situation

Millions of Iranians marked a rare alignment of **Eid al-Fitr** and **Nowruz** under bombardment. Large crowds gathered at holy sites like the Imam Reza Shrine in Mashhad, with many gatherings turning into anti-Western protests.

Funeral ceremonies continue for Iranian officials killed in the strikes, including Intelligence Minister Esmail Khatib and his family.

## What Comes Next

The conflict shows no clear signs of resolution:

1. **US considers Kharg Island occupation** - Could cripple Iran's economy but risk major escalation
2. **Strait of Hormuz security** - Who will police this critical waterway remains unclear
3. **Gulf energy infrastructure** - Continued attacks threaten global oil supplies
4. **Civilian casualties mounting** - Over 13,000 reported dead across all fronts according to some sources

As the war enters its fourth week with no diplomatic off-ramp visible, the risk of broader regional conflagration continues to grow.
