OpenAI has released GPT-5.4, a frontier model featuring a one-million-token context window and an "extreme reasoning mode" for multi-hour tasks. Google introduced Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, an efficiency-focused model that delivers 2.5 times faster response times and 45% faster output generation compared to earlier Gemini versions, priced at a sharply lower rate of $0.25 per million input tokens. This release highlights a growing industry trend towards making powerful AI more accessible and cost-effective for a broader range of users. Additionally, Google announced sweeping Gemini AI upgrades across Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Drive, designed to automate data entry and complex spreadsheet tasks.

**Microsoft Enters Reasoning Race with Phi-4, Introduces Copilot Cowork**
Microsoft has launched Phi-4-reasoning-vision-15B, a compact multimodal model capable of processing images and text, optimized for navigating graphical user interfaces. Furthermore, NVIDIA announced its next-generation Rubin supercomputer platform, engineered to power massive AI workloads and reduce inference token costs significantly.
