# $50 Trillion Missing — The US Financial Coup with Catherine Austin Fitts

> [Play audio stream](http://ubuntu4:8081/download/WTFinance/audio-UaAd4DscZ6s.mp3) — 62.8MB MP3, 192kbps, served from your server.

Catherine Austin Fitts — former Managing Director at Dillon, Read & Co. and Assistant Secretary of HUD under George H.W. Bush — sits down with the WTFinance podcast to map what she describes as a systematic looting of public finances on a scale most people find impossible to believe.

## The Numbers That Don't Add Up

Between 1998 and 2015, the Department of Defense and HUD had **$21 trillion in undocumented adjustments**. Add $29 trillion in bailouts during the financial crisis, $5 trillion injected directly during the "going direct" policy, and quantitative easing on top — and you're looking at **$55+ trillion** drained from the American public. Fitts frames it plainly: "It's like a body with a tapeworm."

The scale isn't about people buying Ferraris. It's about the kind of money that builds a whole new civilisation — and it's being extracted above the level of elected government.

## The Global Control Grid

Fitts argues that the shifting geopolitical landscape — tariffs, dollar reserve pressure, digital payment infrastructure — is one story, not many. The infrastructure for a global digital control grid is being constructed in plain sight. Iran, for instance, needs to be brought into the Western central banking fold. Wesley Clark's "seven countries in five years" plan after 9/11 always had Iran as the final target.

The transition from financial warfare to physical/resource warfare is accelerating. The Straits of Hormuz, China's energy strategy, and the reconfiguration into a North-South American strategy all point to the same endpoint: embedding the control infrastructure globally.

## Mr Global and the Bank of International Settlements

At the centre of Fitts' framework is what she calls "Mr Global" — a network of private financial power operating above elected governments. The Bank of International Settlements (BIS) in Basel plays a key role as the coordination mechanism. The move toward central bank digital currencies (CBDCs) and stablecoins isn't innovation — it's infrastructure for unprecedented visibility and control over how ordinary people spend money.

## What You Can Actually Do

Fitts offers concrete steps, not just analysis:

1. **Come clean** — Get your financial house in order. Know where your money is.
2. **Protect cash** — Support state-level legislation to protect physical currency and put guardrails on programmable money.
3. **Stop financing your own prison** — "We go to work building the control grid. Then we invest our pension funds financing it. Just stop."

Her final message: "The game's afoot. Don't get off the couch. Get off the couch — because you have a vested interest in making sure the Freedom Team wins."
