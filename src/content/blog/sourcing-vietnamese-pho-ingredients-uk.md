Making authentic Vietnamese pho at home in the UK? Here's where to source all your ingredients from UK supermarkets and Asian stores.

## The Pho Recipe

Based on [BBC Good Food's Pho Ga recipe](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/vietnamese-chicken-noodle-soup), you'll need:

### Ingredients Checklist

**For the Soup:**
- Chicken breasts (500g)
- Chicken stock
- Shallots, garlic
- Ginger
- Lemongrass
- Star anise
- Cinnamon stick
- Coriander seeds
- Chinese five spice
- Fish sauce
- Sugar

**To Serve:**
- Rice noodles
- Spring onions, carrots
- Mung bean sprouts
- Fresh coriander & mint
- Lime, chilli
- Crispy shallots (optional)
