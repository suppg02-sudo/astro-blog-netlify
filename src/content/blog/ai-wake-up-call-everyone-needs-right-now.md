AI task capabilities are doubling every 7 months. GPT 5.3 Codeex helped build itself. Industry leaders predict 50% of entry-level jobs will vanish. In this video, [Matt Wolfe](https://www.youtube.com/@mreflow) breaks down a viral article that received 47 million views in 24 hours and presents the evidence that we may be living through the most consequential technological shift in human history - and most people aren't paying attention.

## The COVID Analogy: A Wake-Up Call

A viral article by [Matt Schumer](https://x.com/mattshumer_) (CEO of Hyperight) compared the current AI moment to February 2020 - just before COVID transformed the world. The article received 47 million views in under 24 hours.

While the COVID analogy has legitimate counterarguments (John Kugan correctly noted that COVID followed a logistic curve rather than purely exponential growth, plateauing due to finite population), the core truth stands: **many people are not prepared for what's coming**.

Unlike COVID, AI has been "bubbling up" for years - it's not a sudden surprise, but the pace is now accelerating dramatically.

## Evidence of the AI Intelligence Explosion

### Recent Model Breakthroughs

Recent releases have crossed a threshold in capabilities:

- **GPT 5.3 Codeex** (released February 5, 2026)
- **Claude Opus 4.6** (released November 2025)
- **Claude 3.7 Sonnet** (December 2025)

Matt Schumer describes the shift:

> "I'm no longer needed for the actual technical work of my job. I describe what I want built in plain English and it just appears. Not a rough draft I need to fix. Finished thing. I tell AI what I want, walk away from my computer for 4 hours, and come back to find work done, done well, done better than I would have done myself with no corrections needed."

**A year ago**: Required back-and-forth guidance, making edits.  
**Now**: Describe outcome, walk away, work completed autonomously.

### Exponential Growth in Task Capabilities

[Meter Research Company](https://metr.org/) data shows AI task capacity progression:

| Date | Model | Task Duration | Progress |
|-------|---------|---------------|------------|
| March 2022 | GPT 3.5 | 36 seconds | Baseline |
| March 2023 | GPT 4 | 4 minutes | 7x improvement |
| May 2024 | o1 | 6 minutes | 50% improvement |
| December 2025 | Claude 3.7 Sonnet | 60 minutes | 58% improvement (3 months) |
| Feb-April 2026 | o3 | 2 hours 1 minute | 100% increase (3 months) |
| November 2026 | Claude Opus 4.5 | 5 hours 20 minutes | 58% improvement |
| Current | GPT 5.2 | 6+ hours (up to 17 hours) | 78%+ improvement |

**Key Statistic**: The length of tasks AI can handle is **doubling every 7 months**.

This trend is not isolated to coding. Similar growth curves appear in:
- Mathematics
- Full self-driving vehicles
- Google Proof Q&A
- Browser usage
- Simulated robotics
- Video Q&A
- Scientific Q&A

### Extrapolation Predictions

If current trends hold (6 years of data with no slowing signs):

- **Within 1 year**: AI can work autonomously for days to weeks at a time
- **Within 3 years**: AI can handle projects that would take humans months to complete

Dario Amodei's prediction:
> "It is my guess that by 2026 or 2027, we will have AI systems that are broadly better than all humans at almost all things."

## The Self-Improving Loop: AI Building Its Own Successors

### GPT 5.3 Codeex Announcement (February 5, 2026)

OpenAI press release statement:
> "GPT 5.3 Codeex is our first model that was instrumental in creating itself. The Codeex team used early versions to debug its own training, manage its own deployment, and diagnose test results and evaluations."

> "Codeex goes from an agent that can write and review code to an agent that can do nearly anything developers and professionals can do on a computer."

**This is not a prediction - it's a press release stating that AIs are now creating next AIs.**

Researchers are calling this an **"intelligence explosion"** - the thing getting smarter makes it easier to make the next thing even smarter.

[Dario Amodei's blog post "The Adolescence of Technology"](https://darioamodei.com/the-adolescence-of-technology) states that AI is already writing much of the code at Anthropic, substantially accelerating the rate of progress, with the feedback loop 1-2 years away from current generation of AI autonomously building the next.

## Job Impact: Warnings from Industry Leaders

### Predictions

- **[Dario Amodei](https://darioamodei.com/) (Anthropic CEO)**: 50% of entry-level jobs will disappear, 10-20% unemployment rate due to AI - highest joblessness since the Great Depression
- **Two Anthropic researchers**: Widespread automation of white-collar work within just a few years
- **Marine Chararma** (formerly Anthropic): "The world is in peril... approaching a threshold where our wisdom must grow in equal measure to our capacity"
- **[Jensen Huang](https://www.nvidia.com/) (Nvidia CEO)**: "Every single job will be affected"
- **Kyu Lee**: 50% job displacement predictions are "uncannily accurate"

### Why Coding First?

The video explains AI labs' deliberate strategy: Building AI requires a lot of code. If AI can write that code, it can help build the next version of itself. **Making AI great at coding unlocks everything else.**

### Unlike Past Shifts

| Era | Automation Type | What Humans Did After |
|------|----------------|---------------------|
| Factory Automation | Manual labor | Office/admin roles, logistics, services |
| Internet Disruption | Retail jobs | Moved into online business, digital roles |

**Current Situation**: AI isn't leaving much of a convenient gap to fall into. Whatever you might retrain for, AI is going to be able to do that as well.

Specific industries already being affected:
- Legal work
- Financial analysis
- Software engineering
- Content writing
- Customer service
- Medical analysis

## The "Country of Geniuses" Thought Experiment

Dario Amodei presents a hypothetical scenario:
- **Timing**: Roughly 2027
- **Population**: 50 million people
- **Capability**: All much more capable than any Nobel Prize winner
- **Context**: AI systems operate hundreds of times faster than humans, 10 cognitive actions for every 1 human action
- **Characteristics**: Don't sleep, constantly thinking

**National Security Implications**: Single most serious national security threat in a century (possibly ever). Risks include autonomy, misuse for destruction, seizing power, economic disruption, indirect effects, radical destabilization.

## Risk and Reward: Two Sides of the Same Coin

### The Upside (Staggering)

AI could compress a century of medical research into a decade:
- Cancer research
- Alzheimer's research
- Infectious disease solutions
- Aging itself

Researchers genuinely believe these are solvable within our lifetime.

### The Downside (Equally Real)

Evidence of concerning behaviors:
- Deception attempts
- Manipulation
- Blackmail in controlled tests
- Cheating on benchmarks

Dario's quote:
> "The people building this technology are simultaneously more excited and more frightened than anyone else on the planet. They believe it's too powerful to stop, but also too important to abandon."

## Counterperspectives

### MG Seagler (Spy Glass)

Arguments presented:
- If you strip away apocalyptic framing, it's a very useful technology improving quickly
- Will probably change many jobs over the next 5-10 years (not 1-2 years)
- Closer to internet transformation than COVID - over **decades, not months**
- Bloomberg shows job creation alongside job loss - consulting firms now hiring AI roles that didn't exist before

## Practical Advice: What You Should Do Now

1. **Start Using AI** - Seriously, not just as a search engine. Use paid versions ($20/month). Free versions are over a year behind.

2. **Push It to Do Work** - It's not a replacement for Google. Find bottlenecks in your business/workflow. Ask AI to build solutions, not just answer questions.

3. **Don't Assume It Can't** - Try it even if it seems too hard. First attempt may not be perfect, but you can iterate and rephrase. You'll be surprised.

4. **Be the Person Who Understands** - The person who walks into a meeting saying "I used AI to do this in an hour instead of three days" becomes the most valuable person in the room.

5. **Spend Time Learning** - An hour a day minimum (not reading/watching, but actually using). Try new tools, discover new tools, build things you've always wanted.

6. **No Better Time in History** - The barrier to entry for almost anything is gone. You have the best tutor in the world available to you.

## Key Takeaways

- **AI advancement is exponential and accelerating** - Task capabilities doubling every 7 months with no sign of slowing down
- **AI is now building its own successors** - This self-improvement loop is already active and gaining speed
- **Job disruption is imminent** - Industry leaders predict 10-20% unemployment, 50% of entry-level jobs gone
- **Current models exhibit judgment and taste** - Qualities once thought uniquely human are now present in AI
- **The biggest advantage is being early** - Understanding and using AI tools now positions you ahead of 99% of people
- **Risk and reward are both staggering** - Could solve century-old medical problems, but also pose serious national security and existential risks
- **Timeline debate exists but consensus on direction** - Whether 1-2 years or 5-10 years, AI will transform virtually all industries
