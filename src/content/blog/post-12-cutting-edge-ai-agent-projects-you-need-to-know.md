# 12 Cutting-Edge AI Agent Projects You Need to Know About Right Now

The world of Artificial Intelligence moves at breakneck speed. Every week, developers are releasing new tools designed to automate operations, streamline infrastructure, and revolutionize marketing workflows. If you aren't keeping up, you're likely missing out on systems that could save your team dozens of hours per week.

From visual command centers for operations to AI models that run locally on your phone, the variety of tools emerging is staggering. Based on a recent roundup by ManuAGI, we’ve curated a list of the top trending AI agent projects that are worth your attention right now.

Whether you are a founder, a developer, or a marketer, here are the projects shaping the future of automation.

## **Operations and Infrastructure Management**

Managing complex systems usually means jumping between dozens of dashboards. These new agents aim to consolidate that chaos into conversational interfaces and automated workflows.

### **1. Agent Pulse by Rectify**
*Best for: Founders and Product Teams*

Agent Pulse acts as a visual control center for AI operations. Built on Rectify's Quant agent layer, it replaces the traditional dashboard overload with a conversational interface. Instead of clicking through screens to check monitoring, session replays, support tickets, or cron jobs, you can manage it all through natural language commands. It connects product analytics, code scans, and roadmap data into one workspace, effectively turning SaaS operations into a chat-based command line.

### **2. Metoro**
*Best for: DevOps and Site Reliability Teams*

Metoro (referenced as MRO in recent updates) is an AI site reliability agent designed specifically for teams running Kubernetes infrastructure. It automates the heavy lifting of deployment verification, issue detection, and root cause analysis. By connecting directly to infrastructure telemetry, Metoro can suggest and even execute fixes without requiring code changes. It offers an opt-in layer for data usage, ensuring teams maintain control while speeding up incident response times.

### **3. Deploy Hermes**
*Best for: Engineering Teams*

Releasing software is often fraught with manual checks and operational handoffs. Deploy Hermes is an AI deployment agent that automates these workflows. It ensures that releases move through validation and launch stages smoothly by connecting engineering workflows with deployment operations. For teams looking to increase release velocity without sacrificing safety, this tool reduces the friction of production rollouts.

### **4. Glassbrain**
*Best for: AI Operators and Builders*

As AI agents become more autonomous, understanding what they are doing becomes harder. Glassbrain provides visibility into cognitive workflows. It offers a clear interface to observe how agents process tasks, reason through problems, and execute flows. This is essential for debugging and auditing autonomous systems, bringing a necessary layer of trust and control to AI operations.

## **Creative and Marketing Automation**

Content creation and campaign management are often bottlenecked by resources. These agents aim to scale creative output without scaling headcount.

### **5. KREV**
*Best for: E-commerce Brands and Amazon Sellers*

KREV is a creative engine built for speed. It allows e-commerce brands to generate studio-style product photos, video ads, and performance creatives from simple prompts. By removing the need for external studios or complex editing tools, KREV helps brands iterate faster. In a landscape where ad production speed dictates growth, this tool is a game-changer for Shopify stores and consumer brands.

### **6. Influio**
*Best for: Marketing Teams and Agencies*

Influencer marketing is powerful but difficult to manage at scale. Influio acts as an AI marketing agent that streamlines creator discovery and campaign optimization. It matches brands with relevant creators and tracks performance signals across channels. By making the process data-driven, it helps brands improve reach and conversion without the manual grind of vetting influencers.

## **Development and Collaboration Tools**

Building software is easier when testing is automated and collaboration is seamless. These tools focus on the engineering workflow itself.

### **7. Oguron**
*Best for: QA Teams and Fast-Moving Developers*

Oguron is an AI testing agent designed to take the pain out of QA. It generates automated tests, updates them as the product changes, and helps trace bugs across releases. For teams that want reliable test coverage without hiring dedicated test engineers or constantly rewriting suites, Oguron aligns testing with product updates automatically.

### **8. Mismo**
*Best for: Startups and Product Teams*

Mismo creates a shared workspace for humans and AI agents to collaborate. It centralizes tasks and workflow states, allowing AI "teammates" to assist with planning and execution. This reduces the fragmentation that often occurs when humans and AI work in silos, ensuring that context is shared and projects stay organized.

## **Business Strategy and Planning**

Starting a business involves a lot of paperwork and strategic planning. AI is stepping in to handle the heavy lifting.

### **9. ProAI**
*Best for: Early-Stage Founders*

ProAI is a business planning agent that helps founders generate investor-ready materials. It takes startup inputs and converts them into structured business plans, financial forecasts, and pitch decks tailored to specific industries. This significantly reduces the time it takes to go from a rough idea to fundraising readiness.

## **Local AI and Content Discovery**

Finally, two projects stand out for their focus on accessibility and the open web.

### **10. Tiny Aya**
*Best for: Developers building in multiple languages*

Developed by Cohere, Tiny Aya is a multilingual model designed for local deployment. It is efficient enough to run on edge devices, including phones, while supporting dozens of languages. This is a crucial step forward for privacy and accessibility, allowing developers to build language-aware AI experiences without relying on massive cloud servers.

### **11. Surf**
*Best for: Curators and Communities*

From Flipboard, Surf is a social discovery platform that aggregates content from the open social web—think Blue Sky, Mastodon, and podcasts—into shareable feeds. It helps users organize content into "social websites," giving creators and communities more control over how they discover and interact with information.

### **12. Mercury Edit 2**
*Best for: Writers and Editors*

Rounding out the list is Mercury Edit 2, a system designed for fast text refinement. It assists users in rewriting and improving text workflows, acting as a powerful editor for anyone working with large amounts of copy.

## **Conclusion**

The landscape of AI agents is shifting from simple chatbots to complex, autonomous systems that can manage infrastructure, create marketing assets, and write code. Whether you are a DevOps engineer looking to tame Kubernetes or a founder preparing for a fundraising round, there is likely an agent being built to solve your specific problem.

Keeping an eye on these weekly releases is the best way to stay ahead of the curve. Be sure to check out the projects that resonate with your workflow and start experimenting.
