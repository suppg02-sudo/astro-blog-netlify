<h2>The Scale Problem</h2><p>As AI systems become more autonomous, businesses face a new risk: failure at scale. Minor errors introduced by AI due to gaps between what it was told and what was intended can compound over weeks or months.</p><h3>Key Risks</h3><ul><li><strong>Silent Scaling</strong>: Small errors multiply as AI operates autonomously</li><li><strong>Black Box Complexity</strong>: Model complexity exceeds human comprehension</li><li><strong>Guardrail Gaps</strong>: Difficulty applying controls to advanced systems</li></ul><h3>The Solution</h3><p>Experts recommend implementing robust oversight mechanisms and regular audits.</p>
