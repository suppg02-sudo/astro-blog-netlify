# A Political History of Contemporary Iran

## Executive Summary

Dr. Roy Casagranda delivers a sweeping historical analysis of Iran spanning 125+ years, from the late 19th century through the 2022-2023 women-led revolution. The lecture reveals how Iran's repeated attempts at democracy were systematically undermined by British and American interventions, culminating in the 1979 Islamic Revolution and the current uprising sparked by Mahsa Amini's death.

---

## Key Historical Phases

### The British Oil Conquest (Late 1800s - 1908)

Britain recognized oil would replace coal and needed its own source. British researcher William D'Arcy negotiated a catastrophically lopsided deal with the opium-addicted Qajar ruler: **Britain received 84% of oil profits, Iran only 16%.** The Anglo-Persian Oil Company discovered massive reserves, Khuzestan.

### First Democracy Attempt (1905-1911)

Iranians launched their first bid for democracy in 1906, creating the Majlis (parliament). Britain, fearing renegotiation of oil concessions, **funded the clergy and coordinated with Russia to crush the democratic experiment** by 1911.

### The 1953 CIA Coup

Prime Minister Mohammad Mosaddegh nationalized the Anglo-Iranian Oil Company, ending British exploitation. After shutting down the British Embassy to prevent spy operations, he was overthrown by the CIA's **Operation Ajax**, led by Kermit Roosevelt (Theodore Roosevelt's grandson).

### The 1979 Islamic Revolution

The Shah's tyranny, cancer diagnosis, and departure created a power vacuum. The U.S. preferred an Islamist government over a Soviet-aligned communist one. Ayatollah Khomeini consolidated power and **slaughtered communist and socialist allies** who had helped in the revolution.

### The Iran-Iraq War (1980-1988)

The Reagan administration encouraged Saddam Hussein to invade Iran. The devastating 8-year war killed **750,000+ Iranians and 250,000+ Iraqis**, ending in stalemate.

### The 2022-2023 Women's Revolution

After Mahsa Amini died in morality police custody, Iran erupted in protests led by women with the slogan **"Zan, Zendegi, Azadi" (Women, Life, Freedom)**. Even fully covered women defend uncovered women, united around choice rather than banning hijab.

## Core Themes

### 1. The Persistent Democratic Dream

Iranians have attempted democracy at least **six times**: 1906, 1951, 1979, 1990s, 2009, and 2022-2023.

### 2. External Interference Pattern

- **Britain**: Funded clergy, coordinated invasions, exploited oil (1900s-1950s)
- **United States**: CIA coup (1953), backed Shah's tyranny, encouraged Iraq-Iran war

### 3. Oil as Curse

The discovery of oil transformed Iran into a geopolitical prize, with foreign powers willing to overthrow democracies to control resources.
