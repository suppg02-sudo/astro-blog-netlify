# What is Interactive Content?

> TL;DR: Interactive content is a new kind of reading experience where you dont just scroll — you choose paths, answer quizzes, track goals, and reveal layers of depth at your own pace.

## The Problem with Static Content

Most blog posts are walls of text. You read top to bottom, get what you get, and move on. There is no adaptability. No way to say I already know this, skip ahead or I am new here, go easy on me.

Static content treats every reader the same.

[tip:Research shows interactive content generates 2x more engagement than static content. But until now, it required complex web apps to build.]

## What Changes with Interactive Content?

Interactive content brings three things that static writing cannot:

- **Layered depth** — You see a summary first, then choose how deep to go
- **Active participation** — Quizzes, checklists, and choices keep you engaged
- **Personal paths** — Your experience adapts to what you pick

[reveal:Want the technical details?|Interactive content uses shortcodes in markdown — simple text tags like [quiz:...] and [path:...] that a renderer converts into styled HTML components. No JavaScript framework needed. The state persists in your browser localStorage.]
