## 1. Top Story: OpenAI Launches GPT-5.4 Cyber with Tiered Access Controls

OpenAI released **GPT-5.4 Cyber**, a specialized variant of its GPT-5.4 model fine-tuned for **defensive cybersecurity tasks**, the company announced Tuesday. The model is **not available on ChatGPT** — instead, OpenAI is implementing a new tiered access framework to control who can use it, directly addressing the dual-use risks that come with AI-powered vulnerability discovery. In a blog post, OpenAI said it is releasing the model "in preparation for increasingly more capable models from OpenAI over the next few months." ([Livemint](https://www.livemint.com/technology/tech-news/openai-launches-a-less-restricted-gpt-5-4-cyber-model-but-you-wont-find-it-on-chatgpt-11776214141205.html), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/04/14/openai-model-cyber-program-release))

The launch is a calculated counterpoint to rival **Anthropic**, which recently developed its **Claude Mythos Preview** model — scoring **93.9% on SWE-bench Verified** and **94.6% on GPQA Diamond**, and reportedly finding "thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities across every major OS and browser" — but **declined to release it to individual users** over misuse concerns. ([llm-stats.com](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)) OpenAI's approach is the opposite: release the model, but gate access through vetting and usage controls.

## 2. Headlines

- **Anthropic SDK adds `claude-opus-4-7`, deprecates Claude Sonnet 4 and Opus 4.** Anthropic shipped `anthropic-sdk-python` v0.96.0 today, adding support for a new **Claude Opus 4.7** model and token budget features. Two days earlier, v0.95.0 had already marked Sonnet 4 and Opus 4 as deprecated, signaling an imminent model transition. ([GitHub release notes](https://github.com/anthropics/anthropic-sdk-python))

- **Google DeepMind launches Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS.** Released April 15, the new text-to-speech model supports **direction-based stage directions** for expressive AI narration, positioning it as a direct competitor to ElevenLabs. It's available via the Gemini API and Google AI Studio. ([BuildFastWithAI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/gemini-3-1-flash-tts-google-ai-voice-model-2026))

- **Google DeepMind upgrades Gemini Robotics.** The upgraded Gemini robotics model, which now enables robots to "see, think, plan, and do," is being made available to developers through the Gemini API and Google AI Studio. ([Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4574852-googles-deepmind-launches-more-advanced-gemini-robotics))

- **Stanford's 2026 AI Index Report released.** The report highlights accelerating model capabilities across the industry, notes that both **OpenAI and Anthropic are hurtling toward IPOs later this year**, and documents growing public resentment toward AI, particularly in the U.S. where local governments are beginning to push back. ([IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/state-of-ai-index-2026))

- **Nexar launches BADAS 2.0 incident prediction model.** Trained on **2 million real-world events** (5× more data than BADAS 1.0), the model achieves **99.4% average precision** and ranks **#1 on all four benchmarks**. Its 22M-parameter Flash Lite variant alone outperforms a 2B-parameter foundation model. Built on the V-JEPA2 architecture. ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/nexar-launches-badas-2-0-the-worlds-best-incident-prediction-model-trained-on-two-million-real-world-events-302744732.html))

- **OpenAI Codex v0.121.0 ships with marketplace support.** The update adds plugin marketplace installs from GitHub, git URLs, and local directories, plus richer TUI history, memory controls, expanded MCP/app-server APIs, and hardened devcontainer and macOS sandboxing. ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai))

- **LangChain patches SSRF vulnerabilities across multiple packages.** Releases on April 16 — `langchain-text-splitters` 1.1.2, `langchain-openai` 1.1.14, and `langchain-core` 1.2.30/1.2.31 — all switch to **SSRF-safe transport** for URL-based text splitting and image token counting. A new `langchain-classic` 1.0.4 package was also published with deprecation warnings. ([GitHub release notes](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langchain))
