## New Models & Capabilities

**OpenAI Rolls Out GPT-5.4 and Lighter Variants**
OpenAI has launched GPT-5.4, touted as its "most capable and efficient frontier model for professional work," featuring advanced reasoning, coding, and tool-use capabilities with a significantly expanded context window of up to 1 million tokens. Accompanying this flagship release are GPT-5.4 mini and GPT-5.3 Instant, faster and more cost-efficient models aimed at broader developer workloads and improved conversational flow in ChatGPT.

**Xiaomi Reveals Stealth "Trillion-Parameter" MiMo-V2-Pro**
What was initially known as the stealth "Hunter Alpha" model on the OpenRouter platform has been officially revealed as MiMo-V2-Pro, developed by Xiaomi's AI division, MiMo. This trillion-parameter model, which garnered significant attention during its anonymous testing phase for its impressive specifications, is now accessible via Xiaomi's MiMo API platform alongside multimodal and text-to-speech companion models.

**Google DeepMind Enhances Gemini API with Advanced Tooling**
Google DeepMind has introduced significant enhancements to its Gemini API, launching Multi-tool Chaining and "Context Circulation" mechanisms. These upgrades are designed to streamline the integration of various tools, including Google Search and Google Maps, allowing for seamless data transfer between tools and improving efficiency for complex tasks. Separately, DeepMind also announced the hiring of Jasjeet Sekhon, formerly of Bridgewater Associates, as its new Chief Strategy Officer to focus on developing Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) safely.

## Company Strategies & Developments

**OpenAI Prepares Desktop "Superapp" Amidst Acquisitions**
OpenAI is reportedly developing a unified desktop "superapp" that will combine its ChatGPT chatbot, Codex coding platform, and the Atlas AI-powered web browser. This strategic move aims to integrate its core offerings into a cohesive enterprise solution and counter increasing competition. Reinforcing its capabilities, OpenAI also recently acquired Astral, a startup known for its Python developer tools, to bolster the Codex ecosystem, and Promptfoo, an AI security platform, to enhance agentic security testing.

**Meta's AI Initiatives Marked by Data Security Incident**
Meta has rolled out a new AI support assistant globally across Facebook and Instagram to provide 24/7 help for account and safety issues, aiming to boost user engagement and advertising revenues. However, the company recently confirmed an internal incident where an AI agent's erroneous instruction led to the temporary exposure of a large amount of sensitive company data to some employees. On the infrastructure front, Meta also signed a five-year contract worth up to $27 billion with Nebius Group for AI data center capacity, featuring Nvidia's next-generation GPUs.

**Microsoft Unifies Copilot Efforts and Prioritizes AI Security**
Microsoft is consolidating the commercial and consumer aspects of its Copilot system into a single integrated effort, spanning the Copilot experience, platform, Microsoft 365 apps, and AI models. This aims to provide more coherent and competitive AI experiences. Furthermore, Microsoft announced new Zero Trust for AI tools and guidance to help organizations secure their AI deployments and manage evolving attack surfaces.

## Industry Trends & Governance

**Anthropic Engages in Legal Battle with Pentagon**
Anthropic is suing the U.S. Pentagon after being designated a "supply chain risk" by the Department of War, a move the AI company claims is illegal retaliation for its stance on limiting military use of its AI, particularly for autonomous weapons and mass surveillance. The Pentagon has cited concerns regarding Anthropic's use of foreign workers, including those from China, as part of its justification for the designation. A hearing for temporary relief in the lawsuit is scheduled for March 24.

**US Government Proposes AI Legislative Framework**
The White House has released its recommendations for AI policy and regulations for Congress and federal agencies, aiming to maintain U.S. leadership in AI while addressing societal challenges. The framework seeks to streamline federal regulations and includes objectives such as providing tools for parents to manage children's digital environments and ensuring AI platforms reduce potential sexual exploitation. Several states are also advancing their own AI-related legislation, including bills on AI disclosure, chatbot safety for minors, deepfake protections, and AI use in medical decisions.
