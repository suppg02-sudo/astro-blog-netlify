## The Problem: Inconsistent Plant Care

Jeff Butts admits he never had a natural talent for keeping plants alive. The pattern was familiar: notice drooping leaves, panic, either water everything at once or forget again for three days. Some plants got too much attention, others got almost none.

> "The Raspberry Pi turned plant care into a feedback loop, and that made the whole thing feel less mysterious and much more sustainable."

## The Solution: Data-Driven Plant Care

Instead of relying on memory, Butts built a Raspberry Pi setup to monitor:

- **Soil moisture** - The biggest fix was knowing when NOT to water
- **Temperature** - Exposed drafty spots near vents
- **Humidity** - Revealed drier-than-expected conditions
- **Light levels** - Showed plants weren't getting enough light

### The Hardware Setup

- Raspberry Pi 4 or Zero 2 W as the central hub
- Raspberry Pi Pico satellites for analog sensor readings near each pot
- Capacitive soil moisture sensors
- Temperature/humidity sensors
- Light sensors
- Jumper wires and breadboard for prototyping

## Key Insights

**Overwatering was the real problem.** Moisture sensors revealed that some plants weren't thirsty at all—they were suffering from the owner trying too hard, too late.

**Context matters.** When one plant kept drying out faster than expected, the environmental data provided the answer. When another looked stagnant despite regular watering, the real issue was poor light.

**Confidence through data.** The system turned plant care from guesswork into something measurable and repeatable. Yellow leaves stopped feeling like personal failure.

## Why It Worked

The project targets the exact failure point that keeps many people from succeeding with plants: forgetfulness, overcorrection, and inconsistent routines.

> "My plants are healthier, not because I suddenly became gifted at gardening, but because the system is better at catching what I used to miss."

The Raspberry Pi didn't replace care—it provided the structure that good habits usually provide, even when those habits weren't reliable.
