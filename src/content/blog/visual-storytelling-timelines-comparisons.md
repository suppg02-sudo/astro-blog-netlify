# Visual Storytelling: Timelines and Comparisons

> TL;DR: Timelines show how things evolve. Comparisons show how things differ. Both make abstract ideas concrete and scannable.

## The Evolution of Blog Content

[timeline:Static HTML pages|1995|Blogging platforms emerge|2003|Social media sharing|2007|Long-form content boom|2012|Interactive content era begins|2018|AI-assisted interactive content|2024|Full interactive skill with shortcodes|2026]

## Two Approaches to Teaching

[compare:Traditional: Write everything, hope the reader finds what they need. One size fits all.|Interactive: Layer the content, let the reader choose their depth. Personalised by design.]

## Reveal: Deeper Thinking

[reveal:Why does visual storytelling work?|The human brain processes visuals 60,000x faster than text. Timelines give spatial context (before/after). Comparisons give relational context (same/different). Together they create a mental framework that pure prose cannot match.]

## Test Your Knowledge

[quiz:What does a timeline shortcode show best?|Statistical data|Chronological progression|Opinion polls|2|Timelines are ideal for showing how things change over time — evolution, milestones, roadmaps.]

## Actions

[action:Think of a topic you write about that has a chronological element — draft a mental timeline]
[action:Identify two things you compare often in your writing — plan a comparison block]
